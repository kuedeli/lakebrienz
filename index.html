Essential hiking tips for Lake Brienz

Everything you need to know before you lace up your boots.

⛅

Best time to hike

May–October. Mornings are perfect — fewer crowds and magical light over the lake.

👟

What to bring

  • • Good hiking shoes
  • • Rain jacket (weather changes fast)
  • • Binoculars for chamois spotting
  • • Reusable water bottle
🚤

How to get there

Train from Bern to Brienz (45 min) or boat from Interlaken. Iseltwald is just a 10-minute walk from the boat dock.
